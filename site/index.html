Recruited is an applicant tracking system for small teams hiring their first people. Tell Claude what you're hiring for. It writes the job, the application form, and a listing in each board's own format, each with its own apply link. Applicants fill in the form, and every written answer records how it got there.

What it does

Everything a first hire needs.
Nothing a talent team would.

One role, every board

Board guides for LinkedIn, Indeed, OnlineJobs.ph, Wellfound, Upwork and your own careers page: length limits, how pay is stated, what gets a listing rejected. Each listing gets its own apply link, so two versions on one board are an A/B test.

create_listing · listing_report

Forms that ask the right thing

Must-have questions that mark who missed one, file questions for a setup photo or a speed test, and screening forms sent to each shortlisted applicant through a personal link.

set_application_form · get_screening_link

A ranking you can check

Claude proposes an A, B or C tier for each applicant with a reason, following the rubric you wrote for the role. You decide. A tier you set is never overwritten.

rank_applicants · set_tier

Messages, interviews, offers

Screening invites and rejections drafted for you and sent from your own email. Interview rounds with a scorecard per interviewer. Offers kept as versions, so a counter never erases what you offered.

prep_message · record_scorecard · record_offer

Bring the applicants you already have

Paste an Indeed or LinkedIn applicant export and everyone lands in the job at once, counted against the listing they came through. It previews first and never adds anyone twice.

import_applicants

Or don't talk to it at all

Every job, applicant, answer and file has a screen. Talking is faster for the busywork; the screen is where you look, adjust and decide. Both read the same data.

Provenance

What the form saw.
Not a verdict.

The form counts characters by how they arrived, using the browser's own input events: typed, pasted, dropped, or filled in by something else, like autofill or an auto-typer. It notes a paste that comes right after the applicant left the page. It stores counts and timings, never keystrokes, and the form tells applicants what it records.

Writing style is a separate, weaker signal, and it always says likely. It misfires on second-language writers and people who use grammar tools, so it's a reason to ask a follow-up question, never a reason on its own to pass on someone.

Nothing in Recruited declines anyone automatically, and nothing calls an applicant a cheat.

Yours

Free, open source,
one variable to run.

Recruited is AGPL-3.0. It needs Node and a Postgres database, and DATABASE_URL is the only setting: uploaded files live in the database too. Run it with Docker Compose in one command, or deploy it on Railway with a Postgres beside it.

Connect Claude by pasting your connection URL as a custom connector. Anything that speaks MCP works the same way.

Questions

The ones that come up.

Does it post to job boards for me?

No. None of these boards offers a posting API to a small employer without a partner agreement. Claude writes the listing in the board's format and checks it against the board's rules; you paste it and tell Claude when it's live.

Does it send email from its own server?

No. Messages are drafts until you send them from your own email, through your mail connector or the Open in email button, and then they're marked sent, so each applicant's page shows what they were told and when.

Can it tell for certain that an answer was written with AI?

No, and it never says it can. It reports what the form observed and labels style signals as likely. Treat both as a prompt for a question in the interview.

Is it related to Hired?

They're siblings. Hired is an applicant tracking system for applicants; Recruited is the one for the people hiring. They share a look and a way of working, and they're separate products with no connection between them.
